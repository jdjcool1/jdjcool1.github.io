brief description of DSA - Delron Security Alliance

The Delron Security Alliance (DSA) is an interstellar security and defense organization responsible for protecting allied worlds, technologies, and strategic locations across space. It works alongside the Cosmic Galactic Alliance (CGA), acting as its military and security branch. While the CGA focuses on diplomacy, exploration, and cooperation between civilizations, the DSA handles defense, intelligence gathering, and rapid response to threats, ensuring the safety and stability of alliance territories.

brief description of CGA – Cosmic Galactic Alliance

The Cosmic Galactic Alliance (CGA) is a large interstellar alliance of civilizations, explorers, and defense forces that work together to maintain peace, cooperation, and progress across multiple star systems and galaxies. The organization coordinates exploration, shares advanced technology, and protects member worlds from cosmic threats while encouraging collaboration between different species and cultures.

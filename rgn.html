brief description of RGN - Rift Gate Network

Is an advanced transportation system that uses a compressed alternate dimension called Riftspace to enable near-instant travel across vast cosmic distances. By generating wormholes within Riftspace—where 1 millimetre equals 1 trillion light years in realspace—the network allows ships, data, and energy to move between distant locations almost instantly. Access to Riftspace requires a Riftspace Carrier Signal, which stabilizes entry and ensures safe transit between connected Rift Gates.
